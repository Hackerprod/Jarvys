## Normative root accounting, completion and overflow

Creating the original Central root atomically persists its started identity with **requests=0, executions=1, wakes=0**: the initiating Central turn is charged, not free. Exact duplicate root entry never repeats that charge. Durable progress renews the window only under the qualified WAIT/child-settlement rules above; no terminal root can renew or reopen.

Ending the first USER model turn does not alone complete a request that still owns awaited children. If any awaited direct dependency is pending, unreported, NEEDS_INPUT, or not yet incorporated by the exact Central continuation, the root remains WAITING with those identities retained. Normal root completion requires the native confirmed final assistant receipt for that request and all its awaited direct dependencies terminal, reported and incorporated; the sole explicit-withdrawal alternative is a fresh exact USER cancellation disposition for a provably never-started dependency, not fabricated model incorporation. No unresolved selected continuation/claim may be discarded. Completion atomically marks the Central node terminal and closes root admission/wakes with COMPLETED, conserving counters and evidence. Reply-none children do not block that normal completion: already-started detached children may still finish/report, without renewing or reopening the terminal root. A failed/cancelled root is also terminal and cannot restart from late reports.

For an eligible needs_input event, first persist the exact question/report/wake identity and increment its progress-window wake count. The **49th** retained needs_input wake then triggers budget-failure cancellation of the nonterminal owned root subtree: close admission/resumption, conserve the event/count and STARTED uncertainty, and stop only exact captured native owners outside the transaction. Do not drop that question, refund/replay execution, or apply this overflow result as an artificial cap on terminal-result reporting. Late terminal evidence remains visible; it grants no resumed execution of a stopped/completed root.
